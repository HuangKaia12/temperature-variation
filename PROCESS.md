## Kept
I chose to use colour to represent temperature values.
Mapping temperature magnitude onto colour makes it possible to view thousands of daily readings in one single plot. Rather than drawing many separate line traces, colour‑encoding creates a compact overview showing long‑term temperature patterns, seasonal cycles and gaps in the dataset all at once.

## Rejected
I rejected the default axis ordering where newer years appear at the top and older years sit at the bottom.
This default layout felt counter‑intuitive for reading time‑series data. I flipped the axis so older years are on the top and newer years are on the bottom. This arrangement follows a more natural reading flow for historical data, allowing viewers to read chronologically from top to bottom.
